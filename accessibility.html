Digital Inclusion & Accessibility

Accessibility Statement

We are committed to ensuring that the CS-DPCRU website is fully accessible to all individuals, including people with visual, hearing, motor, or cognitive impairments.

Conformance Standard

This website has been architected to adhere to Level AA of the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 and the Guidelines for Indian Government Websites (GIGW 3.0), formulated by the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY), Government of India.

Key Accessibility Features

  • Screen Reader Access: The portal is compatible with major assistive technologies including NVDA, JAWS, VoiceOver, and Windows Narrator. Semantic HTML5 landmarks (`header`, `nav`, `main`, `footer`, `section`, `article`) provide intuitive structure.
  • Skip to Main Content: An immediate skip link is provided at the very beginning of the document to allow keyboard and screen-reader users to bypass redundant navigation bars.
  • Text Resizing (`A-`, `A`, `A+`): Built-in text scaling controls in the top bar allow users to adjust text magnification dynamically without distorting page layout.
  • High Contrast Mode: A dedicated High Contrast toggle converts color schemes to high-contrast black/yellow/cyan combinations meeting WCAG AAA contrast ratios.
  • Keyboard Operability: All navigation links, buttons, form controls, and carousel toggles are fully navigable via the keyboard using standard keys (`Tab`, `Shift + Tab`, `Enter`, `Space`, `Escape`).
  • Alternative Text on Images: All functional illustrations include descriptive `alt` attributes, while decorative elements are marked with `aria-hidden="true"`.

Screen Reader Information

Users relying on screen readers can navigate this site using following shortcuts:

  • Headings: Press `H` to move between major sections and subsections.
  • Landmarks: Press `D` (or `R` in JAWS/NVDA) to jump across regions like Banner, Navigation, Main, and Content Info.
  • Links: Press `K` or `Tab` to cycle through links.
