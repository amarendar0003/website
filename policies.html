Website Policies

Mandatory legal and institutional frameworks governing access, privacy, copyright, and hyperlinking for the CNLU–GIGA Cyber Security and Data Protection Compliance Research Unit.

1. Copyright Policy

Material featured on this website may be reproduced free of charge in any format or media without requiring specific prior permission, subject to the material being reproduced accurately, completely, and not being used in a derogatory or misleading context.

Where the material is published or issued to others, the source must be prominently acknowledged as CNLU–GIGA Cyber Security and Data Protection Compliance Research Unit (CS-DPCRU). However, the permission to reproduce this material does not extend to any material on this site that is identified as being the copyright of a third party. Authorisation to reproduce such material must be obtained from the respective copyright holders.

2. Hyperlinking Policy

Links to External Websites

At many places in this website, links to other websites or portals are provided. These links have been placed for user convenience. CS-DPCRU is not responsible for the contents and reliability of the linked websites and does not necessarily endorse the views expressed in them. We cannot guarantee that these links will work continuously and we have no control over the availability of linked pages.

Links to CS-DPCRU from Other Websites

Prior permission is required before hyperlinks are directed from any website or portal to this site. Framing of pages from this site within frames of external sites is strictly prohibited; pages belonging to this site must load directly into a newly opened window or tab.

3. Privacy Policy & DPDP Act 2023 Compliance

As a techno-legal research centre specialising in digital privacy and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act 2023), this website adheres to stringent privacy standards:

  • No Tracking Cookies: This website does not deploy persistent commercial tracking cookies, third-party advertising beacons, or profiling telemetry.
  • Information Collection: The website only captures voluntarily submitted information through official enquiry channels. No personal information is captured silently or sold to third parties.
  • Data Retention & Security: Any institutional communications received are safeguarded according to standard data fiduciary responsibilities under Indian law.

4. Terms of Use & Legal Disclaimer

The information posted on this website is for informational, academic, and research purposes only. It does not constitute formal legal counsel, statutory certification, or regulatory approval. While all efforts have been made to ensure accuracy and currency, the Unit assumes no legal liability for errors, omissions, or interpretative consequences.

All queries and disputes arising from the use of this website shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the competent courts in Patna, Bihar, India.
