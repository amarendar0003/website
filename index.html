About Us

Advancing digital trust through law, technology and research.

The CNLU–GIGA Cyber Security and Data Protection Compliance Research Unit (CS-DPCRU) is an institutional initiative described in this brief as bringing together legal scholarship, cybersecurity expertise, technology innovation and regulatory compliance.

As organisations increasingly rely on digital platforms, artificial intelligence, cloud infrastructure and data-driven decisions, the Unit aims to connect legal analysis, technical understanding, institutional preparedness and practical implementation.

Its techno-legal approach is intended to support measurable security, responsible data use and sustainable digital trust.

Our Vision

Secure and trusted digital transformation

To emerge as a leading techno-legal research and capacity-building centre advancing secure digital infrastructure and responsible data governance in India.

Our Mission

Research, frameworks and capacity

Promote interdisciplinary research, develop practical governance and compliance approaches, and strengthen the capacity of public and private institutions.

Our Approach

Connect law with practice

Bring legal obligations, technology safeguards, organisational accountability and risk management into a coherent institutional approach.

Our mission in practice

  • Promote interdisciplinary research in cybersecurity, data protection, digital trust and national security.
  • Develop practical compliance frameworks, assessment methods, governance models and policy recommendations.
  • Build capacity across government, public institutions, businesses, legal practice and technology.
  • Encourage collaboration among academia, industry, regulators, law enforcement and public-sector institutions.
  • Advance secure and accountable adoption of emerging technologies, including artificial intelligence.

Focus Area 01

Cyber Security Operations

Building secure, resilient and cyber-ready institutions through research, preparedness and capacity building.

Governance & risk

Cybersecurity policies, risk identification, mitigation planning, control frameworks and accountability mechanisms.

Security preparedness

Readiness and maturity frameworks, vulnerability-management guidance, access-control review and third-party risk methods.

Incident resilience

Incident plans, escalation and reporting procedures, business continuity, tabletop exercises and lessons-learned methods.

Cybercrime & digital evidence

Research and training on cybercrime investigation, electronic evidence, evidence handling and inter-agency coordination.

Emerging technology security

Research on AI, cloud and connected-device security, deepfakes, digital identity and risk-based technology governance.

Scope: Assessments, policy reviews, risk advisory, incident preparedness and training may be developed subject to capability and approval. Technical security testing, audits and incident response should only be offered where appropriate expertise, infrastructure and authorisations are in place.

Focus Area 02

Data Protection Compliance Operations

Translating data protection law into organisational practice through legal research, compliance frameworks, process assessment and capacity building.

Work may address India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and applicable rules, commencement notifications and other requirements, as relevant to each organisation and activity.

  • Data protection governance, policies and accountability
  • Data mapping, flows, retention and vendor risks
  • Preliminary DPDP readiness and prioritised roadmaps
  • Privacy by design for digital services, AI and analytics
  • Breach preparedness and response coordination
  • Training for legal, compliance, public-sector and technology teams
Potential outputsReadiness reports, frameworks, templates, risk registers and implementation roadmaps, subject to agreed scope.

Guidance supports organisational decision-making; it is not regulatory approval, statutory certification or a guarantee of compliance.
